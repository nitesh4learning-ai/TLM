MM-007aDraft v3.3Suggested from 12 steward decisions

Ownership change at a known site

Stage: match & mergeApplies to: customersOwner: customer master governanceChanges: credit limit, payment terms
1Written in plain English
When a request matches an existing record at the same site but has a different tax ID, and D&B reports an ownership change in the last 12 months, don't merge. Create a new customer and link it to the old one as its successor. Copy the ship-to address and delivery instructions. Send credit limit and payment terms to Credit for a fresh decision. Deactivate the old record after 30 days if it has no open orders.
