# MM-007a Ownership change at a known site

**Autonomy:** B – Agent decides, human approves above limit · **Agent:** Match & merge · **Owner:** Customer master governance

## In plain English

When a request matches an existing record at the same site with a different tax ID, and D&B reports an ownership change in the last 12 months, do not merge. Create a new customer and link it to the old one as its successor. Copy the ship-to address and delivery instructions. Send credit limit and payment terms to Credit for a fresh decision. Deactivate the old record after 30 days if it has no open orders.

## Conditions and actions

| Condition | Action | Decided by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Match score 0.80 or above, address exact, tax ID differs, ownership change within 365 days | Create new customer; link as successor | Agent |
| Credit limit and payment terms | Reset; routed to Credit | Credit agent |
| Old record with open sales orders | Keep active until orders close | Agent |

## Exceptions

No ownership change reported: the request goes to a data steward.
