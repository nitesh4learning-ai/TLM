# AG-003 Citations and audit

**Autonomy:** A – Agent decides · **Agent:** Orchestrator · **Owner:** Data governance

## In plain English

Every decision an agent takes records the policy IDs it used, the inputs it saw and the outcome. Screens show the policy on hover, and the full trail is kept in the audit log.

## Conditions and actions

| Condition | Action | Decided by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Every agent decision | Policy ID, version, inputs, outcome, time | Agent |
| Decision with no matching policy | Logged as a gap; routed to a person | Agent |

## Exceptions

None.
